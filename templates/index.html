Features

All platforms

  • 🗃️M3U files, M3U links and Xtream
  • 🎬Live TV, movies and series
  • 🚅Instant search, setup, updates for even the biggest playlists
  • 🎊Add multiple subscriptions, search and use all of them at once
  • 🌟Favorites, categories and watch history
  • 🔄Choose when to refresh (update) your sources
  • 🪶Lightweight, no ads, no tracking, open source

Mobile & Android TV

  • 🧭Simple and intuitive guided setup to add your subscriptions and playlists
  • 📺Big-screen Android TV layout with full D‑pad navigation
  • 🌬️Fast & optimized for all devices, including low-end devices
  • 🧙‍♂️Rock-stable and smooth playback with ExoPlayer on Android and MPV on iOS

Desktop

  • 🎥Record live TV
  • 🗓️Simple TV guide (EPG) and catch-up
  • 📡Re-stream to other devices on your network
  • 🛠️Custom channels from YouTube, Twitch and more
  • 💾Download movies and catchback VODs to watch offline
  • ⚙️Powered by the full mpv client
